I obtained the 3rd place of the 2026 Lobo Hackathon, a competitive event that challenges participants to develop innovative solutions to real-world problems using technology. The hackathon provided an opportunity to collaborate with a team of talented individuals, brainstorm creative ideas, and implement a functional prototype within a limited timeframe.

We presented EnviroGPT, an accessible AI agent that enables researchers and practicioners to access environmental data with one prompt. The project aimed to simplify the process of retrieving and analyzing environmental data, making it more efficient and user-friendly for researchers and practitioners in the field.
