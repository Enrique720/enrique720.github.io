LoboSec, the University of New Mexico's cybersecurity team, placed 3rd in the 2025 AlamoCTF, a competitive Capture The Flag (CTF) event. The competition tested participants' skills in various areas of cybersecurity, including web exploitation, reverse engineering, cryptography, and forensics.

As LoboSec president and team leader, I led the team through the competition, coordinating our efforts and strategizing to maximize our performance. Our team successfully solved a variety of challenges, demonstrating our technical expertise and problem-solving abilities.

I was also able to obtain the 1st place in the individual category, showcasing my personal skills and knowledge in cybersecurity. This achievement reflects my dedication to continuous learning and improvement in the field.
