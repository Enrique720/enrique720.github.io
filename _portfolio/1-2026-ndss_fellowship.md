For the 2026 Network and Distributed System Security Symposium (NDSS), I was awarded the NDSS Fellowship for my research on the security analysis of Virtual Private Network (VPN) applications. The fellowship recognizes outstanding contributions to the field of cybersecurity and provides support for researchers to attend the conference and present their work. 

This experience allowed me to engage with leading experts in the field, gain valuable feedback on my research, and expand my professional network. The fellowship also provided an opportunity to showcase my work on MVPNAlyzer, an investigative framework for auditing the security and privacy of mobile VPNs, to a broader audience of cybersecurity professionals and researchers.
